AI Engineer (m/f/d) — Cosuno (Berlin / remote-friendly within DACH)

About the role
We are building the construction-procurement platform of record for the DACH
market. We are looking for an AI Engineer to join the small team owning the
LLM-powered features in our product — document understanding, intelligent
search, and automated workflows for our customers' procurement teams.

You will
- Design and ship LLM-powered features end-to-end: from problem framing with
  PM, through prototype, to production rollout with monitoring.
- Own the evaluation discipline for our LLM features. We don't ship anything
  we can't measure.
- Build agentic workflows (LangChain / LangGraph or similar) for multi-step
  document-understanding tasks.
- Partner with platform engineers on the serving path: latency budgets,
  cost budgets, fallback paths.

Required
- 3+ years of production software engineering. You have shipped systems that
  real customers depend on.
- Strong Python.
- Experience designing and shipping LLM-powered features in production —
  not just notebooks. We care about retries, observability, evals, and cost.
- Comfort owning a feature from "we should do X" to "X is running in prod".

Nice to have
- LangGraph or another agent-orchestration framework experience.
- German (we work in English internally but our customers are German-speaking).
- Background in regulated industries or domains with structured documents.

Why us
Construction is one of the last large industries still running on PDFs and
spreadsheets. The work is technically interesting (lots of messy, structured-
ish documents) and the impact is concrete (procurement cycles measurably
shorter).
